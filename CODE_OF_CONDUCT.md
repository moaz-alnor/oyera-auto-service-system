# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration respectful, professional, and focused on improving the project.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not.

## Expected behavior

- Review ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Give technical feedback with enough context to be useful.
- Respect privacy and confidential business information.
- Do not publish credentials, personal data, or security-sensitive details.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms contributors, users, or project security.

Security concerns should be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
